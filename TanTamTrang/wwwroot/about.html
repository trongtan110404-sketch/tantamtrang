DESIGNER

I’m TanDao, a multidisciplinary designer interested in visual communication, experience design and 3D.

My work is driven by concept, research and experimentation. I’m drawn to projects that allow me to explore unfamiliar subjects and translate them into distinctive visual outcomes rather than relying on aesthetics alone.

I enjoy working across different disciplines because each one gives me a different way to develop an idea. Experience design has expanded the way I think about research, interaction and audience, while 3D is becoming an important part of how I want to build more ambitious visual worlds.

Rather than being defined by one fixed style, I’m interested in developing a recognisable creative point of view.

My long-term direction is Art Direction, with a focus on concept-led, multidisciplinary visual projects.
